## 🌍 What Is This?

**AI Career Coach Africa** is a full-stack AI-powered web application that acts as a **personal career advisor for African professionals** — available 24/7, completely free, and built specifically for the African job market.

> 💡 *Think of it like having a brilliant friend who went to Harvard Business School, knows every recruiter in Nigeria, and is always available to help you — completely free.*

Whether you are a fresh graduate, a professional seeking a raise, or someone trying to land a global remote job from Africa — this app was built for **you**.

---

## ✨ Features

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🏠 Career Dashboard
Beautiful dark homepage with rotating African career facts, daily AI-generated tips, stats, and quick-access buttons to all features.

### 📄 CV Reviewer
Upload your CV as PDF or TXT. AI scores it out of 100, identifies strengths and weaknesses, checks ATS compatibility, and rewrites your professional summary.

### 🎤 Interview Coach
Select any job title and experience level. AI generates 6 real interview questions, scores your answers, shows what you did well, and provides a stronger model answer.

### 💰 Salary Advisor
Get accurate Nigerian and African market salary ranges, a market demand rating, top skills that increase pay, and a word-for-word salary negotiation script.

### 💬 Career Chat
Chat with an AI career expert that understands the African job market. Ask anything — from CV tips to remote work strategies.

### 🎯 Job Match Analyzer
Paste any job description alongside your profile. AI scores your match, lists matching and missing skills, and tells you whether to apply.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🗺️ Career Roadmap
Enter your current role and dream role. AI generates a 4-phase personalized plan with actions, skills to learn, milestones, free resources, and a timeline.

### ✉️ Cover Letter Writer
Enter your name, target job, and company. AI writes a complete, tailored, professional cover letter with an email subject line — ready to send.

### 🔍 Skills Gap Analyzer
Enter your current skills and dream job. AI identifies exactly what skills you are missing, ranks them by urgency, and gives you a free learning plan.

### 🔗 LinkedIn Optimizer
AI rewrites your LinkedIn headline and About section, recommends the top 10 skills to add, and provides a recruiter connection message template.

### 💼 Live Job Board
Browse real remote tech jobs powered by the Remotive API. Click any job and get an instant AI fit analysis — match score, matching skills, missing skills, and application advice.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
